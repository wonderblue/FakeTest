# Accounting Treatments (Meridian Playbook)

Practical entries you will see in the lab. Debits left, credits right.

## 1. SaaS revenue — bill in advance

| When | Entry |
|------|-------|
| Bill annual contract | Dr AR 1100 / Cr Deferred 2400 |
| Collect cash | Dr Cash 1010 / Cr AR 1100 |
| Monthly recognition | Dr Deferred 2400 / Cr SaaS Rev 4100 |

## 2. Services revenue — bill when earned

| When | Entry |
|------|-------|
| Deliver & bill | Dr AR 1100 / Cr Services Rev 4200 |
| Earn before bill | Dr Unbilled 1200 / Cr Services Rev 4200 |
| Later bill | Dr AR 1100 / Cr Unbilled 1200 |

## 3. Vendor OpEx invoice

Dr Expense (6xxx) / Cr AP 2100  
Pay: Dr AP 2100 / Cr Cash 1010

## 4. Prepaid software (12-month)

Purchase: Dr Prepaid 1300 / Cr AP or Cash  
Monthly: Dr Software 6300 / Cr Prepaid 1300 (1/12)

## 5. Accrued vendor expense (no invoice yet)

Dr Expense / Cr Accrued 2200  
Next month reverse or true-up on invoice.

## 6. Payroll

Dr Salaries 6100 + Employer taxes 6110 / Cr Accrued payroll 2300 (or cash/clearing)  
Funding: Dr Accrued payroll / Cr Cash 1020

## 7. Fixed assets & depreciation

Capitalize if ≥ $2,500 and useful life > 1 year:  
Dr FA 1400 / Cr Cash or AP  

Monthly: Dr Deprec Exp 6500 / Cr Accum Dep 1450

## 8. Bad debt reserve (estimate)

Dr Bad debt expense 6600 / Cr Allowance 1150  
Write-off specific invoice: Dr Allowance 1150 / Cr AR 1100

## 9. Sales tax (simplified)

Collect on taxable invoice: Cr Sales tax payable 2500 (with Dr AR for total)  
Remit: Dr 2500 / Cr Cash

## 10. Bonus accrual

Dr Bonus expense 6120 / Cr Accrued 2200 or 2300  
Based on YTD achievement estimate; true-up quarterly.

## Matching principle cheat-sheet

| Goal | Technique |
|------|-----------|
| Match expense to period benefited | Accrual / prepaid amortization |
| Match revenue to delivery | Deferred revenue release / unbilled |
| Don’t inflate assets | Expense below capitalization threshold |
| Present AR net realizable | Allowance for doubtful accounts |

## Common close mistakes (spot them)

1. Recognizing full annual SaaS invoice as revenue in billing month  
2. Forgetting to reverse prior-month accruals → double expense  
3. Capitalizing small tools that should be expensed  
4. AR GL ≠ aging because credit memos parked in suspense  
5. Bank “plug” without outstanding item list
