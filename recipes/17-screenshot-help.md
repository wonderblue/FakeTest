# Screenshot Help

**Slug:** `screenshot-help`  
**Category:** browser  
**Level:** starter

## When to use
Errors, confusing UI, flyers, school forms.

## Variables
`{{GOAL}}`, `{{CONSTRAINTS}}`

## Prompt
```
MODE: Ask
GOAL: {{GOAL}}
I pasted a screenshot.
Explain what I'm looking at in plain English.
Tell me the safest next click/action.
CONSTRAINTS: {{CONSTRAINTS}}
Do not ask me to paste passwords.
```

## Done when
You know what to click — or what to avoid.

## Safety
Crop sensitive info from images.
